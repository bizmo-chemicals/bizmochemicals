Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Bizmo Chemicals, Ilayangudi, Sivaganga, Tamil Nadu

Bizmo Chemicals ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it. By placing an order or contacting us through this website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, delivery address, city and pincode, plus the products and quantities you order.
  • Enquiry details: if you use the "Have a Query" form or contact us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever information you share with us (name, phone number, message).
  • Reviews: if you submit a product review, we store your name and review text.

We do not collect passwords, bank details, card numbers or any payment information — all orders are Cash on Delivery or coordinated over WhatsApp.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order (call or WhatsApp).
  • To respond to your queries and feedback.
  • To prevent misuse such as repeated fake orders.

3. How your information is stored

Order and enquiry details are stored in our private business records (Google Sheets) accessible only to the Bizmo team. We take reasonable steps to keep this data secure, including a secured Google account with 2-step verification.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. Your details are shared only where necessary to fulfil your order (for example, with a delivery person) or if required by law.

5. WhatsApp communication

If you place an order or enquiry through WhatsApp, your messages are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this policy.

6. Cookies

This website uses only minimal browser storage (to remember your cart and checkout details on your own device). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request to view, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us at bizmochemicals@gmail.com or 80723 81426 and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
